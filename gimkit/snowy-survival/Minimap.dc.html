Small map, in game

Actual size × 2
3 8 Mbig map
  • 240 × 123 map (up from 190 × 98), bottom-right, 12 px margin. It clears the hotbar and the Answer Questions button.
  • Terrain is muted so the dots carry the colour. Fences and solid objects (rocks, tires, tables) are dark slate; soft obstacles (snow piles, igloos, ice blocks) are a lighter grey-blue; trees are grey-green.
  • Your dot: blue fill, white ring, then an outer ring in your team colour, on a soft white halo.
  • Camera view: a dark 1 px line with a white inner line, so it shows on snow and on Dark Scraps.
  • Strip under the map: cursed and human counts, plus an M hint. It replaces a legend; the colours already match the roster.

  • 720 × 370 map in a 746 × 450 frame. It is centred in the free space right of the mod panel (x 242 to 1088), so the panel stays usable and the frame stays below the energy counter.
  • The game is dimmed by 45%: one full-screen fill, no blur. The mod panel draws above the dim.

  • Names sit right of the dot, or left near the east fence. Dark text on a white halo, tinted by team. Your name is bold and blue.
  • The header carries counts and the close hint; the legend is shown only on the big map.
  • The corner map is hidden while the big map is open.
